Example 3.5.13.

Find an equation for the tangent plane to the surface \(x+ y^2 + z^3 = 1\) at the point \(\left( 0,0,1\right)\)
🔗
\(x+ y^2 + z^3 = 1\) in green and the tangent plane in light blue
🔗
Solution.
The surface is the level surface \(F(x, y, z)=0\) of the function \(F(x, y, z)=x+y^{2}+z^{3}-1\text{,}\) and the point \((0,0,1)\) is indeed on the surface since \(0+0^2+1^3=1\text{.}\) The partial derivatives are \(F_x = 1\text{,}\) \(F_y = 2y\text{,}\) and \(F_z = 3z^2\text{,}\) so the normal vector to the tangent plane is
\begin{equation*} \nabla F(0,0,1) = \left\langle 1, 2(0), 3(1)^2\right\rangle = \langle 1, 0, 3\rangle \end{equation*}
Substituting into the formula for the (implicit) tangent plane:
\begin{equation*} 1(x-0)+0(y-0)+3(z-1)=0 \Rightarrow x+3(z-1)=0 \end{equation*}

Example 1.1.18.

Given the vector \(\vec{v}=\langle-1,1\rangle\text{,}\) compute:
🔗
(a) \(4 \vec{v}\)
🔗
(b) \(-\frac{1}{2}\vec{v}\)
🔗
Solution 1.
Multiplying each component of \(\vec{v}\) by 4:
\begin{equation*} 4\vec{v}= \langle 4(-1), 4(1) \rangle = \langle -4,4 \rangle \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution 2.
Multiplying each component of \(\vec{v}\) by \(-\frac{1}{2}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} -\frac{1}{2}\vec{v}= \left\langle -\frac{1}{2}(-1), -\frac{1}{2}(1) \right\rangle = \left\langle \frac{1}{2},-\frac{1}{2}\right\rangle \end{equation*}

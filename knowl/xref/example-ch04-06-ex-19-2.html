Example 4.6.6.

Set up (but do not evaluate) an iterated integral (using Cartesian coordinates) that gives the net volume bounded by the surface \(f(x, y)=x-y^{2}\) and the \(x y\)-plane, restricted to the disk \(D\) of radius 2 centered at the origin.
🔗
Solution.
The disk can be expressed as \(x^2+y^2\leq 4 \text{.}\) This gives us the bounds \(-\sqrt{4-x^2}\leq y \leq \sqrt{4-x^2}\) and \(-2\leq x \leq 2\) for y and x respectively. Hence
\begin{equation*} \int_{-2}^2 \int_{-\sqrt{4-x^2}}^{\sqrt{4-x^2}} x-y^2 dy dx \end{equation*}

Example 4.3.8.

Evaluate \(\iint_{R}(6-3 x^2-2y) d A\) where \(R=\{(x, y) \mid 0 \leq x \leq 1,0 \leq y \leq 2\}\text{.}\)
🔗
\(f(x, y)=6-3 x^2-2y\)
🔗
Solution.
The region \(R\) is the rectangle \(0\leq x\leq 1\text{,}\) \(0\leq y\leq 2\text{,}\) so we evaluate the double integral as an iterated integral in the order \(dy\,dx\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \iint_{R}(6-3x^2-2y)\, dA = \int_0^1 \int_0^2 (6-3x^2-2y)\, dy\, dx\text{.} \end{equation*}
Evaluating the inner integral with \(x\) treated as a constant:
\begin{equation*} \int_0^2 (6-3x^2-2y)\, dy = \left. (6-3x^2)y - y^2 \right|_0^2 = (6-3x^2)(2) - 4 = 8 - 6x^2\text{.} \end{equation*}
Then integrating with respect to \(x\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \int_0^1 (8-6x^2)\, dx = \left. 8x - 2x^3 \right|_0^1 = 8 - 2 = 6\text{.} \end{equation*}
